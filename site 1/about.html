About Malwai Giddha Hasda Punjab

Keeping the real folk of Malwa alive – one wedding at a time.

Who We Are

Malwai Giddha Hasda Punjab is a Punjabi folk performance group based at Lal Market, Village Baddowal, Ludhiana. Led by Kuldeep Singh, our team performs Malwai Giddha, Punjabi Boliyan and Bhangra at weddings, Jaggo, Nanka Mel, Lohri, melas and stage programmes.

The name says it all – Hasda Punjab means “Punjab that laughs”. Our boliyan are full of humour and love, and our aim is simple: every guest at your function should laugh, clap and dance.

Our Style

We perform the traditional Malwai style – artists in colourful turla pagg, embroidered jackets, kurta and chadra, with live dhol, chimta, kaato and sapp. We mix old village boliyan with new fun lines made for your family.

Where We Perform
