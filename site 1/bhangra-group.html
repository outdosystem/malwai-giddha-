Bhangra Group for Weddings, Parties & Events

Professional Bhangra dance group in colourful Punjabi dress for weddings, parties, college fests and events.

Professional Bhangra Dance Group

Bhangra is the high-energy harvest dance of Punjab and the face of Punjabi culture around the world. Our Bhangra group performs in bright, matching Punjabi outfits – embroidered jackets, colourful turla pagg, kurta and chadra – with live dhol to give your event a grand, festive look.

Bhangra for Every Event

  • Wedding entry, baraat and reception
  • Jaggo and sangeet nights
  • Lohri and Baisakhi celebrations
  • School and college annual functions
  • Corporate events, product launches and hotel openings
  • NRI welcome and family parties

Giddha + Bhangra Combo

Want the complete Punjabi package? Book our Malwai Giddha + Bhangra combo – boliyan and Giddha for the family dance, then a Bhangra performance for the stage. One team, one call, complete entertainment.

Team Size and Timing

Team size and performance time depend on your event and budget. Call us with the date, venue and type of function and we will suggest the right package.
