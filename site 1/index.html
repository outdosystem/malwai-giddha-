About Us

Malwai Giddha Group from Village Baddowal, Ludhiana

Malwai Giddha Hasda Punjab is led by Kuldeep Singh and a team of experienced folk artists from the Malwa belt. Malwai Giddha is the men's folk dance of Malwa – full of witty boliyan, teasing lines for the groom and his family, and the rhythm of dhol, chimta, kaato, algoze and sapp.

For years we have performed at weddings, Jaggo nights, Nanka Mel, Lohri and stage programmes all over Punjab. Our boliyan are clean, family friendly and full of fun, so everyone from Bebe-Bapu to the youngsters joins the dance.

  • Experienced artists in traditional Malwai dress
  • Live dhol, chimta, kaato and boliyan
  • Performance as per your timing and venue
  • Available all over Punjab, Chandigarh & Haryana

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book Malwai Giddha Hasda Punjab?

Call or WhatsApp +91 99155 48346 with your function date, city and venue. We confirm availability and share the package the same day.

Which cities do you cover?

We are based in Village Baddowal, Ludhiana and perform across Punjab – Ludhiana, Jalandhar, Amritsar, Patiala, Bathinda, Moga, Mohali, Chandigarh and every other district – plus Haryana, Himachal and Delhi on request.

Which functions do you perform at?

Weddings, Jaggo, Nanka Mel, Shagan, Milni, Lohri, birthday and anniversary parties, college fests, cultural melas and stage shows.

Do you provide Bhangra along with Giddha?

Yes. You can book Malwai Giddha, Punjabi Boliyan and Bhangra separately or as a combo package for the same function.

How early should I book for the wedding season?

For November to February and peak wedding dates, book 3–4 weeks in advance. Last-minute bookings are accepted if the team is free.
