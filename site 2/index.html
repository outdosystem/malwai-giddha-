The Heritage of Malwa

Discover the Rich Tradition of Malwai Giddha

Rooted in the fertile plains of Punjab's Malwa belt, Malwai Giddha is a cherished traditional men's folk dance celebrated for its poise, wit, rhythmic storytelling, and community spirit. Known affectionately in village lore as the dance of elders and young men gathering in open courtyards, it stands apart for expressive gesture and unhurried grace.

Male performers—dressed in crisp white kurtas, patterned chadra wraps, embroidered waistcoats, and proud crested turla turbans—form an inviting semi-circle. A lead singer opens with poetic Punjabi Boliyan reflecting rural life, harvest joy, gentle humour, and family ties, while the ensemble responds with crisp clapping and acoustic rhythm.

Accompanied exclusively by traditional acoustic instruments including the chimta, single-string tumbi, dholki, algoza, bugdu, and sapp, Malwai Giddha remains a living cornerstone of Punjabi cultural identity.

“ਮਲਵਈ ਗਿੱਧਾ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਰੂਹ, ਬੋਲੀਆਂ ਤੇ ਲੋਕ ਸਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਦੀ ਜਿਊਂਦੀ-ਜਾਗਦੀ ਵਿਰਾਸਤ ਹੈ।”

Malwai Giddha is the living soul of Punjab's folk poetry and traditional acoustic rhythm.

Traditional Folk Instruments & Props

The Acoustic Soul of Malwai Giddha

Every performance comes alive through the unmistakable acoustic resonance of eight iconic Punjabi folk instruments and traditional wooden props.

ਚਿਮਟਾ

Chimta

Long steel tongs fitted with small brass jingle rings that set the sparkling percussive pulse of every Boli.

ਤੂੰਬੀ

Tumbi

High-pitched single-string plucked lute whose resonant twang introduces classic Malwa folk melodies.

ਢੋਲਕੀ

Dholki

Compact two-headed hand drum providing warm rolling bass and crisp turns that anchor the dancers' circle.

ਅਲਗੋਜ਼ਾ

Algoza

Paired bamboo woodwind flutes blown simultaneously—one carrying the drone while the other weaves folk airs.

ਸੱਪ

Sapp

Accordion-like folding wooden lattice clapper snapped open and shut in synchronized unison for crisp rhythmic accents.

ਕਾਟੋ

Kato

Carved wooden squirrel perched atop a tasselled staff whose pull-cord flicks the tail in time with celebratory beats.

ਬੁਗਦੂ

Bugdu

Plucked wooden drum-resonator producing distinctive undulating bass notes unique to rural Malwa folk ensembles.

ਗੜਵਾ

Gadva

Traditional brass water pitcher tapped playfully with a metal spoon or ring to add bright metallic counter-rhythms.
