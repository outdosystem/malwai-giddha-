Curated Cultural Experiences

Our Performance Services

Authentic Malwai Giddha folk shows crafted for family weddings, courtyard Jaggo nights, Lohri bonfires, heritage melas, university stages, and distinguished institutional gatherings.

01 · Signature Ensemble

Malwai Giddha Live Performances

Our flagship live ensemble features traditional male folk dancers, Boliyan vocalists, and live acoustic percussionists performing in full Malwa attire—white kurtas, patterned chadra, embroidered waistcoats, and tall crested turla turbans.

Every performance is tailored to your venue layout—equally resonant in an open village or resort courtyard or on an elevated celebration stage.

Enquire Now
02 · Folk Poetry & Vocal Storytelling

Punjabi Boliyan

Celebrate the richness of oral Punjabi verse with live call-and-response Boliyan accompanied by Tumbi, Chimta, and Algoza. Our vocalists gracefully weave family names, regional pride, and warm cultural humour into traditional couplets.

Ideal for intimate family receptions, welcome dinners, and cultural evenings where guests love listening, clapping along, and sharing in the poetry.

Enquire Now
03 · Grand Wedding Celebrations

Wedding Entertainment

Elevate your Baraat procession, Milni welcome, Sangeet gala, or Anand Karaj reception with an authentic heritage showcase that honours Punjabi roots while delighting guests of all ages.

Our artists invite family elders, uncles, cousins, and friends into the celebratory circle with graceful rhythms and warm hospitality.

Enquire Now
04 · Pre-Wedding Night Traditions

Jaggo Performances

The traditional Jaggo night embodies neighbourhood warmth and joyful anticipation. Our troupe leads courtyard processions with Dholki rhythms, Algoza melodies, Chimta beats, and upbeat Boliyan that keep every guest engaged beneath the evening lights.

Available for home courtyards, heritage havelis, farmhouses, and wedding resorts across Punjab.

Enquire Now
05 · Seasonal Winter Harvest

Lohri Celebrations

Bring the true folk spirit of Punjab's winter harvest to family Lohri bonfires, community club gatherings, and first-Lohri celebrations. Acoustic Dholki, Bugdu, Gadva, and traditional seasonal Boliyan create an unforgettable hearthside atmosphere.

Enquire Now
06 · Melas & Heritage Days

Cultural Festivals

From Vaisakhi heritage melas to district cultural fairs and diaspora heritage weeks, our performers deliver disciplined, high-impact Malwai Giddha sets showcasing traditional props like the Sapp and Kato alongside live folk instrumentation.

Enquire Now
07 · Educational & Youth Heritage

School & College Cultural Events

Inspire students and faculty at annual days, convocation cultural nights, and youth festivals with live demonstrations explaining the history, costumes, and instruments of Malwai Giddha alongside an energetic stage performance.

Enquire Now
08 · Galas & Auditorium Showcases

Corporate & Stage Events

Hosted conferences, hospitality summits, and milestone galas in Punjab and Chandigarh benefit from a polished, punctual cultural opening or evening spotlight that introduces visiting guests to authentic Malwa hospitality.
