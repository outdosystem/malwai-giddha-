In the Heart of Malwa

Malwa's Beloved Folk Dance in Bathinda

Bathinda sits squarely in the cultural heart of southern Malwa, where rural idioms, long folk couplets, and acoustic Dholki gatherings remain deeply cherished by multi-generational families. Audiences here recognize true folk mastery instantly—from the crisp snap of the wooden sapp to the soulful twang of the tumbi.

Our ensemble travels to wedding resorts, village courtyards, and seasonal harvest festivals in and around Bathinda, delivering heartfelt performances rooted in authentic dialect and respect.
