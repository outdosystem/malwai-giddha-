# Malwai Giddha — Traditional Punjabi Folk Heritage Website

A multi-page cultural showcase website dedicated to **Malwai Giddha**, the traditional men's folk dance of the Malwa region of Punjab, India.

## Technology Stack

- **HTML5** (Semantic, multi-page markup written directly in HTML)
- **CSS3** (`assets/css/style.css` and `assets/css/responsive.css`)
- **Bootstrap 5.3** & **Bootstrap Icons** (via CDN)
- **Google Fonts**: *Playfair Display*, *Poppins*, *Noto Sans Gurmukhi*
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (`assets/js/main.js` for sticky header, hero carousel enhancements, reveal animations, mobile drawer, video modal, gallery category filtering, and lightbox viewing)

## Project Structure

- `index.html` — Full-length homepage (14 curated cultural sections)
- `about.html` — Heritage, cultural roots, Boliyan, costumes & instruments
- `services.html` — Live performances, weddings, Jaggo, Lohri & festival programs
- `gallery.html` — Filterable masonry gallery with interactive lightbox
- `contact.html` — Contact cards, map placeholder & PHP-ready enquiry form
- `cities.html` — Directory of Punjab cities served
- `cities/` — Dedicated SEO city pages (`ludhiana.html`, `patiala.html`, `bathinda.html`, `sangrur.html`, `moga.html`, `amritsar.html`)
- `assets/css/` — `style.css` and `responsive.css`
- `assets/js/` — `main.js`
- `assets/images/logo.png` — Official Malwai Giddha brand logo
