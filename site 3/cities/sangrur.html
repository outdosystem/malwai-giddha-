Sangrur & Surrounding Villages

Poetic Boliyan & Acoustic Elegance in Sangrur

Celebrated across Punjab for its poets, folk singers, and deep respect for Punjabi language, Sangrur cherishes performances where the lyrics of each Boli shine as brightly as the dance. Our travelling artists craft ensembles that highlight clear vocal storytelling alongside crisp Chimta and Dholki accompaniment.

We gladly travel across Sangrur, Dhuri, Sunam, and Lehragaga for family weddings, Jaggo processions, Lohri nights, and college cultural programs.
