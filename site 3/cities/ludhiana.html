Malwai Giddha in Ludhiana

Bringing traditional Punjabi folk artistry, acoustic instruments, and lively Boliyan to Ludhiana's wedding palaces, farmhouse lawns, and cultural institutions.

Celebrations in Ludhiana

Authentic Malwa Folk Rhythm for Ludhiana Gatherings

Situated along the northern edge of the Malwa belt, Ludhiana hosts grand family weddings along Ferozepur Road and Pakhowal Road alongside vibrant university and civic cultural festivals. Our travelling folk ensemble brings pure Malwai Giddha into these contemporary venues without losing a single note of village warmth.

Whether welcoming the Baraat outdoors under marigold garlands or commanding an auditorium stage near Sarabha Nagar and PAU, our troupe performs live with Chimta, Tumbi, Dholki, Algoza, Sapp, and Kato.
