Malwai Giddha in Amritsar

Bringing Malwa's iconic men's folk tradition to Amritsar's destination weddings, heritage havelis, and cultural festival stages.

Across Punjab's Cultural Regions

Malwa Folk Elegance in the Cultural Capital of Majha

As one of India's premier destination-wedding and cultural tourism cities, Amritsar welcomes guests from around the world who wish to experience the full breadth of Punjabi culture. Inviting a traditional Malwai Giddha troupe lets hosts showcase an authentic regional art form rarely seen outside the Malwa plains.

Our travelling artists perform at boutique heritage properties, hotel lawns along GT Road and Airport Road, and cultural auditoriums across Amritsar.
