The Heritage of Malwa

Discover the Rich Tradition of Malwai Giddha

Rooted in the fertile plains of Punjab's Malwa belt, Malwai Giddha is a cherished traditional men's folk dance celebrated for its poise, wit, rhythmic storytelling, and community spirit. Known affectionately in village lore as the dance of elders and young men gathering in open courtyards, it stands distinct for its expressive gestures and unhurried grace.

Male performers—dressed in crisp white kurtas, patterned chadra wraps, embroidered waistcoats, and proud crested turla turbans—form a lively semi-circle. One singer leads with poetic Punjabi Boliyan reflecting rural life, harvest seasons, humour, and family bonds, while the chorus answers in unison with vibrant clapping and rhythmic beats.

Accompanied entirely by acoustic folk instruments such as the gleaming chimta, single-string tumbi, dholki, algoza, bugdu, and sapp, Malwai Giddha remains a living cornerstone of Punjabi cultural identity.

“ਮਲਵਈ ਗਿੱਧਾ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਦੀ ਰੂਹ, ਬੋਲੀਆਂ ਤੇ ਲੋਕ ਸਾਜ਼ਾਂ ਦੀ ਜਿਊਂਦੀ-ਜਾਗਦੀ ਵਿਰਾਸਤ ਹੈ।”

Malwai Giddha is the living soul of Punjab's folk poetry and traditional acoustic rhythm.

Where Every Beat Tells a Story

Three timeless pillars define the grace and authenticity of a traditional Malwai Giddha gathering.

01 · Rhythm & Stance

Traditional Folk Dance

Performed by male artists in vibrant circle formations, Malwai Giddha highlights synchronized shoulder lifts, crisp rhythmic footwork, and expressive hand movements that mirror each lyrical verse.

02 · Oral Poetry

Punjabi Boliyan

At the heart of the circle is the oral artistry of Punjabi Boliyan—witty couplets and elongated folk verses celebrating hospitality, harvest joy, wedding banter, and life in Malwa villages.

03 · Living Legacy

Cultural Heritage

Preserving hand-crafted rural instruments, traditional turban tying, embroidered waistcoats, and community gatherings passed lovingly from generation to generation across the Malwa heartland.

Traditional Folk Instruments & Props

The Soul of Malwai Giddha

Unlike modern amplified stages, authentic Malwai Giddha comes alive through the unmistakable acoustic blend of eight iconic Punjabi folk instruments and wooden props.

ਚਿਮਟਾ

Chimta

Long steel tongs fitted with small brass jingle rings that set the crisp, sparkling percussive pulse of every Boli.

ਤੂੰਬੀ

Tumbi

High-pitched single-string plucked lute whose resonant twang introduces and accents classic Malwa folk melodies.

ਢੋਲਕੀ

Dholki

Compact two-headed hand drum providing warm, rolling bass and sharp rhythmic turns that anchor the dancers' circle.

ਅਲਗੋਜ਼ਾ

Algoza

Paired bamboo woodwind flutes blown simultaneously—one carrying the drone while the other weaves soaring Punjabi folk airs.

ਸੱਪ

Sapp

Accordion-like folding wooden lattice clapper snapped open and shut in dramatic unison for crisp visual and rhythmic impact.

ਕਾਟੋ

Kato

Carved wooden squirrel perched atop a tasselled staff whose pull-cord flicks the tail in time with celebratory beats.

ਬੁਗਦੂ

Bugdu

Plucked earthen or wooden drum-resonator producing distinctive undulating bass notes unique to rural Malwa folk ensembles.

ਗੜਵਾ

Gadva

Traditional brass water pitcher tapped playfully with a metal spoon or ring to add bright metallic counter-rhythms.
